**Who Sets Your Table?**
*Genesis 1:27-29, Genesis 47:19-25, Deuteronomy 8:2-3, Luke 4:3-4*

---


# 1. Socialism makes the state your __table__.

- Socialism says the **group** is more important than the **__individual__**.

---

- The government cannot give what the government does not first __take__.

---

- "All citizens are transformed into hired employees of the __state__." (Lenin, 1917)

---

- The state provides for your lifestyle, demands your loyalty, and __defines__ your life.

<img src="https://cdn.subsplash.com/images/9KT38Z/_source/a129c6f9-a036-4ba1-bbcf-8a993e12e45f/image.png" alt="My Notes.png" />{note}
---

# 2. The garden: sin first came through the temptation for __provision__.

- God gave them **identity**, gave them **dominion**, and **__provided__** for them.

---

- God has provided everything the state seeks to __provide__.

---

- Providing for themselves cost them their life, their provision, their dominion, and their __freedom__.

<img src="https://cdn.subsplash.com/images/9KT38Z/_source/a129c6f9-a036-4ba1-bbcf-8a993e12e45f/image.png" alt="My Notes.png" />{note}
---

# 3. Egypt: Pharaoh's table came through the fear of __death__.

- "You have saved our lives... we will be __servants__ to Pharaoh." (Genesis 47:25)

---

- The grain Pharaoh handed out was first __taxed__ from the people.

---

- A new king who did not know Joseph turned servants into __slaves__.

<img src="https://cdn.subsplash.com/images/9KT38Z/_source/a129c6f9-a036-4ba1-bbcf-8a993e12e45f/image.png" alt="My Notes.png" />{note}
---

# 4. Egypt: Pharaoh's table came through free __stuff__.

- They craved the good things of Egypt, but forgot Egypt's __price__.

---

- Socialism prospers by playing on the **fear of death** or the **__free stuff__**.

<img src="https://cdn.subsplash.com/images/9KT38Z/_source/a129c6f9-a036-4ba1-bbcf-8a993e12e45f/image.png" alt="My Notes.png" />{note}
---

# 5. God's table: bread alone is not __enough__.

- Pharaoh fed them to **keep** them. God let them hunger to **__free__** them.

---

- Sons always have a seat at the Father's __table__.

<img src="https://cdn.subsplash.com/images/9KT38Z/_source/a129c6f9-a036-4ba1-bbcf-8a993e12e45f/image.png" alt="My Notes.png" />{note}
---

# 6. The wilderness: Jesus refused the devil's __table__.

- Adam was a son who disobeyed. Israel was a son who disobeyed. Jesus was a son who __honored__.

---

- Provision is tied to __identity__.

---

- Jesus defeated **free stuff** in the wilderness, and defeated **__fear of death__** on the cross.

---

- Whoever sets your table will try to set your __loyalty__.

<img src="https://cdn.subsplash.com/images/9KT38Z/_source/a129c6f9-a036-4ba1-bbcf-8a993e12e45f/image.png" alt="My Notes.png" />{note}
---

# Altar

- Where have I allowed others to provide for me in place of God?

---

- What from Egypt am I still craving? What is it costing me?

---

- Am I living like a slave waiting to be fed, or a son trusting his Father?
