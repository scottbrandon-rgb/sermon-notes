**Warn From the Wall**
*Ezekiel 33:1-11, Ezekiel 13:10-16, Philippians 1:27-28*

---


# 1. The watchman __warns__ from the wall.

- Even when people are guilty, the silent watchman shares the __blood__.

---

- **Standing by** is still **__standing with__**.

---

# 2. The false prophet __whitewashes__ the wall.

- Silence isn't peace. It's __plaster__.

---

- Have we traded our **convictions** for our **__comforts__**?

---

- A dog that won't __bark__ can't protect the farm.

<img src="https://cdn.subsplash.com/images/9KT38Z/_source/a129c6f9-a036-4ba1-bbcf-8a993e12e45f/image.png" alt="My Notes.png" />{note}
---

# 3. The church __stands__ on the wall.

- The watchman speaks where God has __spoken__.

---

- **Culture** doesn't define truth. It **__reveals__** what people believe about truth.

---

- Eli said the right words and still didn't use the __authority__ he had.

<img src="https://cdn.subsplash.com/images/9KT38Z/_source/a129c6f9-a036-4ba1-bbcf-8a993e12e45f/image.png" alt="My Notes.png" />{note}
---

# 4. Judgment has already come in __Jesus__.

- Is your judgment **__waiting__**, or is your judgment **__paid__**?

---

- Don't __whitewash__ the wall. __Warn__ from the wall.
