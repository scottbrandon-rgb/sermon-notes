*1 Corinthians 6:9-11, Romans 1:24-32, Matthew 19:4-6*

---


# 1. Why should we care? Homosexuality is the warning light, not the starting __line__.

- Homosexuality isn't where the fall __starts__. It's how you know the fall has __started__.

---

- "Any human society is free to choose either to display great energy or to enjoy sexual freedom; the evidence is that it cannot do both for more than one __generation__." (J. D. Unwin, 1934)

---

- The bottom isn't doing it. The bottom is __approving__ it. (Romans 1:32)

---

- If we give them human rights, we lose our __religious__ rights.

---

- Same verse. Same Bible. You don't get to keep one and toss the __other__. (1 Timothy 1:9-10)

- Crime → __Condition__ → Created

- Woe to those who call evil __good__ and good evil. (Isaiah 5:20)

---

# 3. What do we say? We destroy __arguments__, not people.

- "Jesus never addressed it." Homosexuality is not the topic, __marriage__ is. (Matthew 19:4-6)

---

- "Paul meant pederasty." Paul didn't reach for a word about boys. He reached for __Leviticus__. (Leviticus 18:22)

---

- "Love is love." Love seeks to __reconcile__ what is already divided.

---

- God's design for marriage: **Procreation.** **__Illustration__.** **Sanctification.**

---

- Marriage doesn't erase desire. It gives desire a __home__. (1 Corinthians 7:2)

---

- You cannot be set apart for God by the very thing God has called __sin__.

---

- We don't have to win their argument. We just have to stand on God's __design__.

# 4. Is there hope? Who you were is not who you __are__.

- The world says, "This is who you are." God says, "That is who you __were__."

---

- **Salvation** is a matter of identity. **Sanctification** is a matter of **__imitation__**.

---

- You were __washed__. You were sanctified. You were justified. (1 Corinthians 6:11)

---

- Truth without love is __destructive__. Love without truth is __deceptive__.

---

- Don't be __deceived__. Don't be __ashamed__. Be __free__.
