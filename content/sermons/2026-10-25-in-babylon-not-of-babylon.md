**In Babylon, Not of Babylon**
*Daniel 1:1-8, Matthew 13:24-28, Philippians 2:5-7*

---


# 1. Language: Babylon wanted to change what they __said__.

- When you use their terms, you start using their __meanings__.

---

- We are not at liberty to __redefine__ what God has defined.

---

- We must learn to **speak to** Babylon without allowing Babylon to **__speak for__** us.

<img src="https://cdn.subsplash.com/images/9KT38Z/_source/a129c6f9-a036-4ba1-bbcf-8a993e12e45f/image.png" alt="My Notes.png" />{note}
---

# 2. Literature: Babylon wanted to change the way they __learned__.

- We follow __one__ book.

---

- We don't move the **foundation** to straighten the wall. We straighten the wall according to the **__foundation__**.

---

- Plenty of people are free in body, but __captive__ in mind.

<img src="https://cdn.subsplash.com/images/9KT38Z/_source/a129c6f9-a036-4ba1-bbcf-8a993e12e45f/image.png" alt="My Notes.png" />{note}
---

# 3. Lifestyle: Babylon wanted to change the way they __lived__.

- The danger isn't always what the culture **demands**. Sometimes it's what the culture **__offers__**.

---

- Whoever sets your __table__ will try to set your loyalty.

<img src="https://cdn.subsplash.com/images/9KT38Z/_source/a129c6f9-a036-4ba1-bbcf-8a993e12e45f/image.png" alt="My Notes.png" />{note}
---

# 4. Loyalty: Babylon wanted to change what they __stood for__.

- Babylon can set things **on** us. It cannot set them **__in__** us.

---

- They changed his name. They never changed __whose__ he was.

<img src="https://cdn.subsplash.com/images/9KT38Z/_source/a129c6f9-a036-4ba1-bbcf-8a993e12e45f/image.png" alt="My Notes.png" />{note}
---

# 5. Daniel __served__ Babylon. He never __belonged__ to it.

- Rescued **out** of Babylon. Sent **__back__** into Babylon.

---

- Babylon wants your mind. Christ __gives__ you his.

---

- The danger isn't that we're living in Babylon. The danger is when Babylon begins __living__ in us.
