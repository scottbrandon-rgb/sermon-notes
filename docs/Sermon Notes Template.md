SERMON NOTES FORMAT GUIDE
This block is hidden in Obsidian and ignored by the notes site. Leave it in or delete it.

FRONT MATTER (top of file)
- series: the series name, shown above the title.
- date: written as YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2026-11-15). The site shows it as "Sunday, November 15, 2026."
- reminder: optional. One or two sentences for the "A reminder" box at the top of the notes. Leave blank for no box.
- reminder_link: optional. A full web address (https://...) that becomes a button under the reminder.
- reminder_label: optional. The button's words (e.g., Go to the devotionals). Shows "Learn more" if left blank.

TITLE AND SCRIPTURE (right after front matter)
- **Sermon Title** in bold on its own line.
- *Book 1:1-10, Book 2:1-5* in italics, separated by commas. These become the "Today's scripture" buttons.

MAIN POINTS
- Start with "# 1. ", "# 2. ", and so on.
- An optional short label ending in a colon or question mark, followed by a space, shows above the heading:
  "# 1. Language: Babylon wanted..." or "# 1. Why should we care? Homosexuality is..."
- No label is fine too: "# 5. Daniel __served__ Babylon."

RESPONSE SECTIONS
- A heading with no number ("# Altar") becomes a "Respond" section with a "My response" box.

SUBPOINTS
- Start each line with "- ".
- The "---" dividers and blank lines are optional. The site ignores them.

BLANKS
- __word__ makes a fill-in-the-blank. More than one per line is fine.
- A blank can sit inside bold: **__word__**

EMPHASIS
- **bold** and *italics* (use italics for book titles).

REFERENCES
- Anything in parentheses at the END of a line shows as a small reference under the point:
  (Isaiah 5:20) or (J. D. Unwin, 1934) or (*After the Ball*, 1989)
- To keep a parenthetical aside as part of the sentence, add a period after the closing parenthesis.

SCRIPTURE BLOCKS
- "> [!scripture] Romans 1:24-32" on its own line places a Scripture card at that spot (usually right under a point's heading).

NOTE BOXES
- Keep the image line ending in {note} at the end of each section that should get a note box.
